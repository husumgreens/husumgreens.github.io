How to Buy This Lot — FSBO Buyer's Guide

Washington State does not require real estate agents for property sales. A buyer and seller can complete the entire transaction through a title and escrow company. FSBO saves $10,000–$20,000 in agent commissions on a $199K sale. The following is a step-by-step guide specific to Klickitat County, WA.

$199,000 Sale Price
$2,687 State + County REET
~$305 Recording Fees
~$194,500 Estimated Net to Seller

  1. Prepare the Property Information Package

    Before making an offer, the seller will assemble the following for you:

    • Legal description — from the deed or Klickitat County Assessor's records
    • Parcel number — needed for the REET affidavit
    • Plat map — shows lot boundaries
    • CC&Rs / HOA documents (if any for Husum Greens)
    • Utility confirmation letters for existing connections
    • Klickitat County Assessor — Parcel Lookup — (509) 773-3715
    • Klickitat County Planning — (509) 773-5703 — zoning confirmation

  2. Seller Disclosure Statement (Form 17C — Vacant Land)

    Washington law (RCW 64.06) requires the seller to complete Form 17C (Vacant Land) disclosing known material conditions. After delivery, the buyer has 3 business days to rescind based on the disclosure.

    Key disclosures include: utilities, zoning, environmental conditions, easements, and HOA membership.

    • RCW 64.06 — Seller Disclosure Law
    • Form 17C (Vacant Land) available from any title company or WA DOR

  3. Sign a Purchase and Sale Agreement

    Once both parties agree on terms, you sign a written Purchase and Sale Agreement (required by RCW 65.04 — Washington's Statute of Frauds).

    Key terms: purchase price ($199,000), earnest money, closing date (typically 30–45 days), contingencies, and what is included (lot only; architect plans are optional, priced separately).

    Do not use NWMLS Form 21 without a licensed broker — it is NWMLS-licensed. Use a title company's blank form or have an attorney draft the agreement.

  4. Open Escrow with a Title Company

    Submit the signed Purchase and Sale Agreement to a title/escrow company serving Klickitat County to open escrow. The buyer submits earnest money to escrow. The title company handles title search, insurance, document preparation, and funds disbursement.

    • Frontier Title & Escrow — Bingen WA & Goldendale WA — (509) 783-8828 — physically serves Klickitat County
    • Columbia Gorge Title — WA office: 41 Russell Ave, Stevenson WA — (509) 427-5681 — columbiagorgetitle.com
    • Columbia Title Co. — Hood River, OR — (541) 386-2313 — confirm Klickitat County coverage by phone
    • Klickitat County Auditor — (509) 773-4001 — can refer you to currently active local agents

  5. Buyer Due Diligence Period

    During the inspection period (typically 10–20 days per the P&S agreement):

    • Arrange and pay for a boundary survey (if desired)
    • Lender orders appraisal (if financing)
    • Review title commitment from the title company
    • Confirm utility connections with providers
    • Review CC&Rs and any HOA documents

  6. Closing — REET and Deed Recording

    The seller (grantor) pays Washington Real Estate Excise Tax (REET) under RCW 82.45.060. At $199,000:

    • State REET (1.10% on sales under $525K): $2,189.00
    • Klickitat County local REET (0.25%): $497.50
    • Total REET: $2,686.50

    Recording fees: $303.50 for the first page + $1.00 per additional page (Klickitat County fee schedule, effective July 27, 2025). No eRecording — must submit in person or by mail to Room 203, Klickitat County Auditor.

    • WA DOR — REET Rates & Affidavit Form
    • Klickitat County REET Page
    • Klickitat County Recording — Fee Schedule
    • Klickitat County Treasurer (REET payment) — (509) 773-4664, M–F 9am–3pm
    • Klickitat County Auditor (deed recording) — (509) 773-4001 — 205 S. Columbus Ave., Goldendale, WA 98620

  7. Proceeds Disbursement

    At closing, the escrow company pays off any liens, pays the REET to the County Treasurer, pays recording fees to the County Auditor, pays title insurance premiums and escrow fees, then wires net proceeds to the seller. The title/escrow company coordinates all of this automatically as part of the standard closing service.

    Estimated seller costs: REET $2,687 + recording ~$305 + title/escrow ~$1,400 = ~$4,400 total, versus $10,000–$12,000 in listing agent commissions on the traditional route.

  8. Buyer's Agent

    If you're working with a buyer's agent, the seller may negotiate contributing to your agent's commission (~2.5–3%). Ask during negotiation — there is no obligation either way. RCW 18.85.151(1) explicitly allows property owners to sell their own property without a license.
